Source. Fund list and quotes from the public Nasdaq ETF screener, refreshed each weekday after the US close. Prices are end-of-day and unadjusted.
Listed. The fund's first trading day, taken from the earliest bar in its price history. Prospectus inception can precede it by a few days, and a fund that changed sponsor or exchange may show the later of the two dates rather than its original launch.
Derived fields. Sponsor, category, region and leverage are inferred from the fund's name, not from a filings database — the screener does not publish them. They are accurate enough to filter on and wrong often enough that you should confirm against the fund's own fact sheet before acting. Expense ratios, AUM and holdings are not available from this source and are deliberately absent rather than estimated.
