Source. Fund list and quotes from the public Nasdaq ETF screener, refreshed each weekday after the US close. Prices are end-of-day and unadjusted.
Fee. Total annual operating expense from the fund's own prospectus, as tagged in SEC XBRL. Net of fee waivers where the fund reports a net figure, gross otherwise — the modal says which. Known for 4,198 of 5,261 funds; prospectuses are filed annually, so a fund that has not filed recently may be missing or slightly behind a fee change.
Holdings. Click any fund for its ten largest positions, from its SEC Form N-PORT filing. These are quarterly and lagging — each drawer shows its own report date, typically three to six months back — so they describe how a fund was positioned, not how it is positioned today. Around 1,200 funds file no N-PORT at all: commodity and crypto trusts and unit investment trusts report differently, and most of them hold one asset. Swap-based funds report notional, so their positions can sum to more than 100%.
Listed. The fund's first trading day, taken from the earliest bar in its price history. Prospectus inception can precede it by a few days, and a fund that changed sponsor or exchange may show the later of the two dates rather than its original launch.
Category. Where a fund files holdings with the SEC, its category comes from what it actually holds: ≥90% equity is equity, ≥90% debt is fixed income, ≥30% derivatives is derivative income, and any real mix is Multi-asset with the split shown. A chip with an outline was set that way; hover any chip for the source. Funds without a filing — and funds that hold other funds, whose filings cannot say what the underlying funds hold — fall back to the name.
Derived fields. Sponsor, region and leverage are inferred from the fund's name, not from a filings database — the screener does not publish them. They are accurate enough to filter on and wrong often enough that you should confirm against the fund's own fact sheet before acting. Expense ratios, AUM and holdings are not available from this source and are deliberately absent rather than estimated.
