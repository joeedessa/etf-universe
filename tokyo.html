Source. The Japan Exchange Group's published list of TSE-listed ETF issues, refreshed with the nightly job. Trust fee is the annual management fee stated by JPX, before tax, and is known for every fund here.
What is missing, and why. There is no price, daily move, one-year return, fund size or holdings on this page. No free source publishes quotes for Tokyo codes — the Nasdaq screener rejects them, and the SEC feeds behind the US page's holdings and expense data cover only funds registered in the United States, which these are not. Those columns are absent rather than estimated.
Why a separate page. Merged into the US table these funds would be blank under half its columns and would break sorting on price and return. Fee, manager, index and listing date are directly comparable across the two; nothing else is.
