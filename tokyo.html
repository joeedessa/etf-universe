Sources. The list, index, listing date and pamphlet come from the Japan Exchange Group's published table of TSE-listed ETFs. Size, NAV, fee, returns, risk, distributions, asset class, region and the prospectus come from the Investment Trusts Association's fund library (投信総合検索ライブラリー), which publishes each fund's own filings. The two are joined by fund name; the join was validated against ISINs published by the funds' managers.
Returns are NAV total returns as the library reports them — one, three and five years, where the fund is old enough. Fee is the trust fee including consumption tax, so it runs about 10% above the pre-tax figure JPX prints.
Asset class and region are official, from the library's own classification — unlike the US page, where category is inferred from holdings or names.
26 funds have no library record, by construction rather than by omission: WisdomTree's commodity ETFs are Jersey-domiciled, SPDR S&P 500, SPDR Gold and ABF are foreign JDRs, and the physical-metal products are trusts rather than investment trusts. They show JPX's pamphlet and nothing else.
Not on this page: holdings. Only one manager publishes them in machine-readable form. Not investment advice. Nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell any security.
