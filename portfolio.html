What this is. A calculator over the same public data as the main table. It applies weights you choose and reports the arithmetic. It does not recommend allocations, rank portfolios, or model risk, and nothing here is investment advice.
Blended fee is the weighted average of each fund's expense ratio, over the portion of the portfolio whose fee is known — the tile says how much that is. Annual cost applies it to the portfolio value you enter.
Weighted 1-year is a hypothetical: today's weights multiplied by each fund's trailing one-year return. It is not a backtest — it assumes you held exactly these weights all year with no rebalancing, contributions or withdrawals, and it says nothing about the future.
Look-through combines each fund's published top-10 positions, weighted by its share of the portfolio. Since only the top 10 are available, it explains part of the portfolio, not all of it — the panel reports the covered share. Holdings come from SEC N-PORT and lag by a quarter or more.
Not investment advice. Nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell any security.
